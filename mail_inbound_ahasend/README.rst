This module receives email for Odoo's aliases from an
`AhaSend <https://ahasend.com>`__ inbound route, instead of a mail
server that Odoo polls. AhaSend posts each inbound email to Odoo within
seconds of its arrival. Odoo's own mail gateway then handles it as any
other incoming email: a reply lands on the record it answers, and an
email to an alias creates a record, such as a lead or a ticket.

Attachments and inline images are kept. AhaSend signs every delivery,
and Odoo refuses a delivery whose signature does not match.

Configuration
=============

To configure this module, you need to:

1. Set up the AhaSend account in *Settings > General Settings >
   AhaSend*, as the *AhaSend* module (``mail_ahasend``) describes. The
   API key needs the scopes ``routes:write:all`` and
   ``routes:delete:all``.
2. Point the MX records of the domain of Odoo's aliases to AhaSend, as
   the domain details in the AhaSend dashboard show.
3. In AhaSend, set the message data retention of the account to 1 day or
   more: AhaSend discards inbound email at 0 days.
4. In the *Inbound email* box, check the *Inbound domain*. It is filled
   in with the alias domain of the company.
5. Click *Connect inbound email*. Odoo creates a route in AhaSend that
   posts all email for that domain to Odoo, with attachments and
   headers, once per email.

To create the route in the AhaSend dashboard instead, turn on developer
mode and use *Manual route setup* in the *Inbound email* box: copy the
*Route endpoint URL*
(``https://<your-odoo>/mail/inbound/ahasend/<database>``) into the
route's endpoint URL, turn on attachments, headers and deduplication by
Message ID, and copy the route's secret into *Route secret*.

Usage
=====

Once connected, email to Odoo's aliases arrives in Odoo by itself. There
is no incoming mail server to configure, and no delay until the next
poll.

An email that matches no alias and no existing record is dropped and
logged, as Odoo does with such email from a mail server.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

-  AhaSend sends one delivery per email, with its To and Cc headers. An
   email that reached an alias only through Bcc has no trace of that
   alias in its headers, so Odoo can route it only when it replies to a
   known message.
-  AhaSend's spam score is not used yet: every email that reaches the
   route goes to Odoo.
