Mail tracking for AhaSend

This module connects the mail tracking of mail_tracking with AhaSend, a European transactional email service.

Odoo sends its mail through AhaSend over SMTP. AhaSend then reports what happened to each email through a webhook: delivered, deferred, bounced, failed, suppressed, opened, clicked, unsubscribed or marked as spam. The module turns these reports into mail tracking events, so the tracking status in Odoo shows what happened after the email left Odoo.

It also adds a button to fetch the status of an email from AhaSend, and buttons on contacts to check, add and remove an address in the AhaSend suppression list.

Configuration

To configure this module, you need to:

  1. In AhaSend, verify your sending domain and create SMTP credentials. Set them up as the outgoing mail server in Odoo.
  2. Set up the AhaSend account in Settings > General Settings > AhaSend, as the AhaSend module (mail_ahasend) describes. The API key needs these scopes: messages:read:all, suppressions:read, suppressions:write, suppressions:delete, webhooks:write:all and webhooks:delete:all.
  3. In the Delivery tracking box, click Connect tracking. Odoo creates the webhook in AhaSend and stores its signing secret. Disconnect tracking deletes it again. With a Sending domain set, the webhook and the suppression actions are limited to that domain.

To create the webhook in the AhaSend dashboard instead, turn on developer mode and use Manual webhook setup in the Delivery tracking box: copy the Endpoint URL (https://<your-odoo>/mail/tracking/ahasend/<database>) into the webhook’s endpoint URL, send all events or at least the message events, and copy the webhook’s secret into Webhook secret.

The full setup guide is at https://ahasend.com/docs/guides/odoo.

Usage

The mail tracking screens of mail_tracking now show what AhaSend reports for each email, such as Delivered, Bounced or Opened. These statuses are not known from a plain SMTP server.

Opens and clicks that AhaSend detects as bots are ignored.

When a webhook was missed, open the tracking record and click Check AhaSend to fetch the current status of the email. AhaSend keeps message data for a limited time, which depends on your plan and retention settings.

On a contact, the AhaSend buttons next to Email bounced let you:

  • check whether AhaSend suppresses the address,
  • add the address to the AhaSend suppressions,
  • remove the address from the AhaSend suppressions.

Known issues / Roadmap

  • Events of emails sent by mass_mailing are not linked yet. A separate module, like mail_tracking_mass_mailing, can add that.
  • AhaSend has no email validation API, so there is no equivalent of the mailbox validity check of mail_tracking_mailgun.
